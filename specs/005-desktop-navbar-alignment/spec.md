# Centre the desktop navbar menu

Date 21/11/2026

App version `0.1.0-alpha.8`.

At widths of 768 CSS px and above, the centre of the horizontal menu is midway between the right edge of the logo and Alpha group and the left edge of the action icons. The outer groups keep their existing positions. Below 768 CSS px, the hamburger remains on the right and opens and closes as before.
