# Three names for Codewave Studio’s UK rental alpha

Screened on 05/11/2026. For commercial branding, **take LetMallow forward first**. It works for rooms and whole homes and has a less directly overlapping trade mark finding than the other two. RentDapple is the creative favourite for the demo, but an AI software trade mark application makes it a weaker long-term bet. None of the names has full UK trade mark clearance.

Before a commercial launch, repeat searches in the IPO register for UK rights and international marks designating the UK. Search exact, spaced, phonetic and related forms. Review the actual goods and services, especially advertising/marketplaces (35), real estate (36) and hosted software (42), plus downloadable software (9) if offered. Check trading names and unregistered rights. If a name is still preferred, a UK Business & IP Centre is a free starting point for an expert search discussion. **Visual and editorial rules.** Use an original wordmark, symbol, interface wording and owned or licensed photographs. Draw broad inspiration from the reference’s lightness and spaciousness. Make rent, location, availability, free contact and sponsored placement easy to distinguish. Pair dark body text with pale backgrounds and include a visible keyboard focus. Show saved, selected and premium states with words or symbols as well as colour. WCAG AA calls for at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text and 3:1 for large text, subject to the published exceptions.
